The mood board is a unstructured list of inspiration i collected in preparation of the project.

(also mostly german as this began before the decision to make everything in english)

## **=== GRAPHICS (Models, Textures) ===**

Mood Board aus third party Assets / Godot / Blender / Screenshots - NO GAMEPLAY

Was für ein Stil?

* Lowpoly
* “Pixelart”
* Plastic
* Toys
* Modern rendering features like field of view, raytracing, lighting, (ss)-reflections, skybox, tone mapping kombiniert mit low poly
